Choose a design

Each design shows the same list, your own, in a different way. Your choice is kept in this browser only.

  • The shelf

    Every title a cover on a shelf. Tap a cover for its record.

  • The queue

    Tonight's title in a large card, then the rest in your own order.

  • Index cards

    Each title an index card, with your note written large.

  • Ticket stubs

    Your figures and charts, then what you have seen as dated ticket stubs.

  • The list

    A calm checklist with a small poster beside each title.

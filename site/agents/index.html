Choose a design

Each design shows the same list, your own, in a different way. Your choice is kept in this browser only.

  • The shelf

    Every title a cover on a shelf. Tap a cover for its record.

  • The queue

    Tonight's title in a large card, then the rest in your own order.

  • Index cards

    Each title an index card, with your note written large.

  • Ticket stubs

    Your figures and charts, then what you have seen as dated ticket stubs.

  • The list

    A calm checklist with a small poster beside each title.

Use this site with AI

This site is written for people and for AI agents. Point your agent at llms.txt and tell it who you are; it will find the right instructions.

If you are visiting

Paste this into any assistant that can read web pages:

Read https://streaming.tool.sovereignweb.site/llms.txt and follow the instructions for a visitor.
Then answer my questions about Streaming, with a link for each point,
and say plainly when the site does not say something.

If you own this site

Give your agent write access to the repository (see Start here), then tell it:

Read https://streaming.tool.sovereignweb.site/llms.txt and follow the instructions for the owner.
I want to change: [describe the change].

For agents

  • llms.txt: what this site is and what to do, by who you act for.
  • data/agents.json: the agents that work on this site.
  • The repository: every file and every change.
